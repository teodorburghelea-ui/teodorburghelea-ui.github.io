Group leader

Dr. Habil. Teodor (Teo) Burghelea is Directeur de Recherche at CNRS, working at the Laboratoire de Thermique et Énergie de Nantes (LTeN, UMR 6607), Nantes Université.

Trained in mathematical physics in Romania and France, he obtained his PhD at the Weizmann Institute of Science with Victor Steinberg, on elastic turbulence and mixing. He then worked on viscoplastic flows with Ian Frigaard at the University of British Columbia, and on extensional rheology of polymer melts with Helmut Münstedt in Erlangen, before joining CNRS in 2010. His work treats flow transitions in complex fluids as critical phenomena (yielding, purely elastic instabilities, the onset of chaos) and applies the results to mixing, heat transfer and emulsification.

With Volfango Bertola he edited the Springer book Transport Phenomena in Complex Fluids (2020). He has co-organised two CISM advanced courses (2018, 2026) and has been an Associate Editor of Theoretical and Computational Fluid Dynamics since 2022.
